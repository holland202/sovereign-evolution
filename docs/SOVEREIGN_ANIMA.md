# Sovereign Anima — Machine Identity Manifold

**Status:** Concept established June 2026. Implementation planned.

## The Idea

Every physical machine develops a unique operational signature over time.
Not just averages — a geometric identity. The specific way it vibrates,
heats, responds, recovers.

Sovereign Anima gives each machine its own identity manifold.
Scars accumulate over its lifetime.
The machine's history is readable in its geometry.
When the manifold starts collapsing — the machine is dying.

## Three Components

**Machine Identity Manifold**
A rank-constrained operator learned from the machine's own telemetry.
Not a class of machines. THIS specific pump, THIS specific valve.

**Temporal Scar Biography**
Every mechanical stress event leaves a scar.
The accumulation tells you the machine's history —
fatigue, wear patterns, resilience.

**Death Prediction via Manifold Collapse**
Before catastrophic failure, geometric identity becomes unstable.
Spectral norm approaches critical values.
Scar density spikes abnormally.
Sovereign Anima detects this collapse in advance.

## Why This Is Novel

Current predictive maintenance monitors variables.

The machine has a geometry.
That geometry changes over time.
When the geometry starts dying, the machine is dying.
