## Scope of Evidence

This repository documents an edge-native governance prototype and related research framing.

It does **not** establish:

- recursive self-improvement
- general intelligence or autonomy
- production readiness for critical infrastructure defense
- results that have been independently reproduced by third parties
- that prototype latency or absorption figures generalize beyond the single-device development setting

Successful local tests and prototype runs do not by themselves establish operational capability or scientific claims beyond the documented scope.
