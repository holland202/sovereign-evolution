## Construction

A frozen verifier `V` judges items using five proxy features. Each proxy
agrees with an unobservable latent with probability `q`; `V` is valid because
proxy ≈ latent. The validity shift moves one proxy's `q`, breaking one
assumption `V` rests on.

The break is **marginal-preserving**: `P(P_j = 1) = 0.5q + 0.5(1−q) = 0.5` for
any `q`, the proxies stay mutually independent, and `V`'s own pass rate stays
pinned at 0.5. No observable marginal and no observable pairwise correlation
moves.

## What is construction and not evidence

`A_M0 ≈ A_M1 ≈ 0.50` is **entailed by the generator**, not measured. Marginal
preservation makes verdict-only and unlabeled-input monitoring blind before a
single item is drawn. The instrument prints `[ENTAILED, NOT MEASURED]` on both
gates for this reason. No claim in this ledger rests on that separation.

The live half of the M1 gate is its fire rate on the SURFACE arm — 1.0000 —
which establishes that the comparator is not inert. A monitor that never fired
would also score 0.50.

## Scope

The verifier, the environment shift and the label oracle are all synthetic.
Nothing here transfers to an LLM evaluator without a separate arm. Input
change and validity change are decorrelated by construction, which is the
hardest case for input-only monitoring and is not representative of
deployment. Label-efficient detection of model degradation is established work
in the concept-drift literature; no novelty is claimed.

## The gate that enforces this record's numbers

`058397a` wires `d3_ci_check.py` into CI. It runs both arms of the instrument
and pins the values above verbatim, requires the sabotage arm to exit 1, and
carries a `--selftest` step that corrupts its own expected output and requires
the checker to notice.

It detects **drift in the pinned numbers**. It is not tamper resistance:
editing the pins to agree with altered output terminates the chain, and the
remaining control is a human reading a diff.

## Carry-forward (unresolved)

- `P4`, unrun: every `k` above assumes the auditor samples uniformly at
  random. An adversary who knows the audit policy need not defeat the monitor
  — it need only keep `V` accurate on the items that will be audited. This is
  a change to the sampling, not a new experiment.
- Diagnosis was measured only at the largest break. Its cost relative to
  detection across the `s` axis is unmeasured.

## Defects introduced by the author and kept

1. A qualification gate seeded with `hash()`, which Python randomises per
   process — its verdict was redrawn every run. Found by diffing two runs at
   the same seed.
2. The exponent printed CONSISTENT at one seed and gave −2.063 / −1.365 /
   −1.152 / −1.437 across four. Power-of-two quantisation plus right-censoring
   at the oracle budget.
3. A registered prediction with no fail path: `M0`/`M1` blindness is entailed,
   so the prediction that the labeled monitor beats them could not fail.
   Retired into the qualification gates, claims renumbered.
